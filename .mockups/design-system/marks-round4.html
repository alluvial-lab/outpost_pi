Direction set by operator: "a beacon communicating in the expanse of space." The mark should feel like a lone lit outpost and a signal crossing a void — not a diagram. B's block-cursor DNA survives as the beacon element itself (the cursor is the tower of light); C's communication DNA survives as the transmission. All four lean on negative space as the expanse.
