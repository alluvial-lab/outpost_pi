Outpost-Pi — palette & mark options

Outpost-Pi is an independent Alluvial-Lab product and gets its own identity from scratch — the same exploration-led process Patchbay got (mocks → tokens), not a shared family spine. Amber-phosphor instrument-panel is Patchbay's locked territory and is deliberately excluded here so the two products don't read as twins. Three worlds below; each owns its full neutral system, mode priority, and accent — all pairings WCAG-AA verified in both modes. S/NC's recent exploration is referenced only as process precedent.

0 — Mark directions orthogonal to palette

The current mark (geometric π + dot) is byte-identical to upstream remote_pi's art and must be replaced. Three directions — pure geometry (no font dependency), Android safe-zone friendly, each colored by whichever accent wins below.

1 — Night Watch signal blue · cool · dark-first

The relay's night console. Deep cool charcoal with a faint blue cast; one signal-blue accent that means "control channel open." The calmest, most tool-like world — closest in spirit to the current π-dot identity (blue retained, matured), fully distinct from Patchbay's warm amber. Light mode is overcast-cool, not white. Risk: nearest to "safe developer tool" — its restraint is the personality.

2 — Field Manual map-red on paper · light-first

The outpost's logbook. Survey-map paper, hard ink, and the red of a beacon marked on a chart — the only light-first world of the three, and the only one with a warm-red accent. Reads like an expedition document: deliberate, annotated, human. Pairs naturally with mark 3 (pennant) or 1. Farthest from every sibling identity. Risk: red-as-accent must stay disciplined (interactive + beacon semantics only) so it never reads as "error."

3 — Phosphor Beacon terminal green · dark-first

The beacon is lit. Terminal phosphor muted to something editorial — graphite with a breath of green, not gamer RGB. The product story maps the accent onto presence itself: green means your outpost is reachable, the agent is alive. Distinct from Patchbay (warm amber) by temperature and by neutral family. Pairs with mark 2 (beacon) or 1. Risk: proximity to success-green semantics — accent owns brand + interactive; status greens stay in tinted chips.
